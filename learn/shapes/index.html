Shapes

Parejo

Straight sided cigars; aka standard or straight cigars. This is the most common shape.

Torpedo

Tapered at both the head & the foot (pointy head).

Presidente

Aka Salomon — taper at the head & the foot (nipple foot).

Perfecto

Closed at both ends with a bulge in the center.

Pyramid

Tapered head with a wider foot.

Culebra

Two or three cigars braided together.
