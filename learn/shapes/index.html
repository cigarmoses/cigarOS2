Shapes

Parejo
Straight sided cigars; aka standard or straight cigars. This is the most common shape.
Torpedo
Tapered at both the head & the foot (pointy head).
Presidente
Aka Salomon – taper at the head & the foot (nipple foot).
Pyramid
Aka pyramide, piramide; a cigar that is tapered to a point at the head and blossoms toward the cylindrical foot.
Perfecto
Approximately 4–6 inches long, tapered at both ends, with a rounded head and bulbous at the center.
Culebra
Spanish for “snake”; describes the braiding of 3 loosely filled, thin cigars held together by string. Originated in Cuba to control rollers’ cigar consumption and served as workers’ daily ration.
