Anatomy

WRAPPER exterior leaf, visible color of cigar

A high-quality tobacco leaf wrapped around the finished bunch and binder of a handmade, premium cigar. Wrapper leaves are the most expensive type of tobacco. The Spanish term is capa.

BINDER leaves that hold interior filler together

The binder (banda in Spanish) holds the filler tobacco together to form the bunch. Some cigars use multiple binders to add complexity.

FILLER tobacco leaves used in the body of the cigar

Premium cigars typically contain long-filler tobacco (whole leaves). Machine-made cigars use short-filler tobacco.
