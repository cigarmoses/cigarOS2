Vitolas

Robusto

Short, thick, and very common. Great “default” size.

Toro

Longer than a Robusto with a similar ring — a balanced smoke.

Churchill

Long format cigar for a slower, extended session.

Corona

Classic traditional size — slimmer ring, elegant feel.

Torpedo

Tapered head for a more focused draw and concentrated flavor.

Gordo

Big ring gauge cigar — cooler smoke and lots of volume.
